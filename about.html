How it began

A studio born from a love of stones

Just-Jewels was founded in 2020 by Monica Gobane in her small apartment studio in Gaborone. What began as a hobby, wrapping raw crystals in copper wire quickly became a movement.

Word of her handcrafted pieces spread through markets and social media, and today Just-Jewels is a beloved independent brand known for its commitment to ethical sourcing, sustainable packaging, and deeply personal designs.

What we stand for

Our Values

💎

Ethical Sourcing

Every gemstone and metal we use is responsibly sourced from verified suppliers who share our commitment to environmental and human dignity.

🤲

Handcrafted Always

We never use mass-production techniques. Each piece is hand-formed, shaped, and finished by our small team of passionate artisans.

🌿

Eco Commitment

Our packaging is 100% recyclable and our studio runs on solar energy. We strive to leave the smallest possible footprint on the earth we draw from.

✨

Unique by Design

No two natural stones are identical. We embrace this — every piece has subtle variations that make it truly one-of-a-kind, just like its owner.

Behind every piece

Our Craft Process

01

Sourcing

We personally visit suppliers and markets to select only the finest natural stones — choosing each one for its unique colour, texture, and energy.

02

Designing

Each piece begins as a sketch. We consider how the stone's natural shape can guide the design rather than be constrained by it.

03

Crafting

Using hand tools, wire, and flame, our artisans form each piece. The metal is annealed, shaped, textured, and polished all by hand.

04

Packaging

Every piece is presented in our signature eco-boxes, wrapped in recycled tissue and sealed with a wax stamp ready to become a treasured gift.
