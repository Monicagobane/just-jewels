How it began

"A studio born from a love of stones"

Just-Jewels was founded in 2020 in a small studio in Gaborone. What started as a hobby — wrapping raw crystals in copper wire — quickly grew into a beloved independent brand. Every piece is still hand-formed, one at a time, by our small team of passionate artisans.
